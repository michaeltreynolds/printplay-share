# PrintPlay Share

**Share board games the way people share 3D models, except the output is a print order instead of an STL.**

A free, non-commercial place for people to share board games they've **designed themselves**, so anyone can print a
copy, play it, and build on the idea. Like 3D-model sharing sites, every game carries a Creative Commons-style
license chosen by its creator, which says whether others may adapt it and whether they must credit the original.

A designer uploads card and token art and describes each component:
its size, quantity, front and back, and the stock it needs. PrintPlay Share packs everything onto print sheets with an
easy-to-cut layout: pieces share cut lines, cuts run straight across the sheet, and little is wasted. It then produces
a print-ready PDF plus a short order sheet, for example "13x19, 130 lb gloss cover, double-sided, cut on marks",
that you can take to FedEx Office or send to a print-on-demand shop.

Example project: a small social-deduction bluffing game inspired by the mechanics of the classic HOAX (Eon, 1981),
remade with original art and wording so it can be shared openly.
